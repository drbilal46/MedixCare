Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This page explains how privacy is presented within the MedixCare healthcare website concept and clarifies the limits of this portfolio demo.

Portfolio Demo Notice: MedixCare is not a real healthcare provider. This website is a front-end portfolio concept and does not collect or store real patient information.
On this page Overview Information Forms Cookies Security Third Parties Changes Contact

1. Overview

MedixCare is a fictional healthcare website concept created for portfolio and demonstration purposes. It is designed to show how a modern healthcare interface may look and function.

This Privacy Policy is therefore a demonstration document. It does not represent the privacy practices of a real hospital, clinic, doctor, healthcare organization, or medical service.

2. Information We Collect

The current MedixCare portfolio demo does not operate a backend database and does not intentionally collect or store personal information from website visitors.

Demo information

Forms displayed on the website are interactive demonstrations. Information entered into these forms is processed only within the browser interface and is not transmitted to a real server.

3. Forms and Appointments

The contact and appointment forms are included to demonstrate user interface and user experience design.

  • No real appointment is created.
  • No real patient profile is created.
  • No medical record is created.
  • No submitted form is sent to a real healthcare provider.
  • No personal information is intentionally stored by this demo.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The current portfolio version does not intentionally use cookies, advertising trackers, analytics systems, or other tracking technologies for collecting visitor profiles.

If this website is later developed for a real organization, its cookie and tracking practices should be updated to reflect the actual technologies being used.

5. Data Security

Because the current MedixCare concept does not operate a backend data-storage system, it does not maintain a database containing patient information.

A production healthcare website would require appropriate technical, organizational, and legal safeguards before handling sensitive personal or medical information.

6. Third-Party Services

The current portfolio demo does not depend on external healthcare providers, payment processors, appointment systems, or patient-management platforms.

Any future third-party integrations would need to be disclosed in an updated privacy policy and implemented according to applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Policy

This demonstration Privacy Policy may be updated if the MedixCare concept is expanded or connected to additional technologies.

Any production version should maintain a clear record of meaningful changes and provide users with appropriate notice.

8. Contact

For this portfolio concept, the contact page is available for demonstrating the website's communication interface.

Visit the Contact page to explore the demo experience.
