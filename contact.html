Common questions

Before you reach out

A few simple answers built into the demo experience.

Can I book an appointment here?

Yes. The appointment page demonstrates the complete booking interface, but it does not create a real appointment.

Are the doctors real?

No. Doctor names, profiles, availability, and related information are fictional portfolio content.

Does this form send an email?

No. The form is front-end only and displays a confirmation message without sending any data.

Is MedixCare a real clinic?

No. MedixCare is a healthcare website concept created for portfolio and demonstration purposes.
